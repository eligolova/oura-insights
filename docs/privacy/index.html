Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 April 2026

OuraInsights is a personal health analytics project. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles data when it connects to Oura and, in later versions, related context providers such as weather services.

Who this project is for

OuraInsights is intended as a personal-use application for its owner. It is not a public social platform and it does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

What data may be processed

  • Oura account authorisation data needed to connect the app to Oura
  • Oura health data such as sleep, readiness, activity, heart-related metrics, and similar personal wellness records
  • Later versions may also process contextual data such as weather and location information

How data is stored

OuraInsights is designed to store app data on-device. Oura access credentials and tokens are kept in the device keychain. Imported wellness data is stored locally within the app.

How data is used

Data is used only to support the app’s features, such as displaying sleep and readiness summaries, syncing Oura records, and producing personal analytics inside the app.

Network access

OuraInsights only contacts external services that are necessary for its features. At present that means Oura APIs and, in later versions, approved weather APIs. The app does not sell personal data and does not send analytics events to third-party tracking services.

Data sharing

OuraInsights does not share personal data with third parties except where data must be sent to the connected provider in order to operate the service requested by the user, such as communicating with Oura during sign-in or sync.

Retention

Data is retained locally until it is removed by the user, the app deletes it, or the app is removed from the device. Future versions may add more direct in-app controls for deleting local data.

Your choices

  • You may disconnect Oura from the app.
  • You may stop using the app at any time.
  • You may remove the app and its locally stored data from your device.

Medical disclaimer

OuraInsights is not a medical service and does not provide medical, diagnostic, or treatment advice.
